[h1]Child Education[/h1]
Since 1.4 the game tracks the education of the direct heir only. Nothing tells you when the other heirs' children and grandchildren need one, so they grow up on the balanced education the game gives every child.

This mod brings back the alert for children who could get a better education, and can pick their education for you.

[b]Requires:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692202776]Community Mod Framework[/url] (the alert bar and the mod menu).

[h2]Alert[/h2]
[list]
[*]An alert on the Community Mod Framework alert bar when a child is on the balanced education.
[*]Its tooltip names the children. Clicking it opens their cards one after another.
[*]Checked once a month. Children under 3 are left alone, as in the game.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Open the mod menu, or right-click the education button in a child's character window.
[list]
[*][b]Who counts:[/b] the heirs in the first places of the line of succession with their children and grandchildren, or every child of the ruler's family at court.
[*][b]Places in line:[/b] how many places of the line of succession count.
[*][b]Include women:[/b] count girls too.
[*][b]Future royals only:[/b] count only children who stay in the crown estate once the first heir rules. The heir's cousins and their children become nobles then.
[*][b]Auto-education:[/b] off, by traits, or administrative, diplomatic or military for everyone. Educations you chose yourself are kept.
[/list]

[h2]Paid education[/h2]
A table of the child traits that change education, with a box for boys and one for girls. Children with a checked trait get the expensive in-depth education, paid as in the game. If the country can't afford it, they get the auto-education choice and are moved to the paid one once it can.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Overrides [i]character_lateralview.gui[/i] (the character window) for the right-click, so mods that change the same file may conflict.
[*]Also carries a fix for the Community Mod Framework alert tooltip, which sometimes shows "_name" and "_tooltip" instead of the text. It will be removed once the framework has its own fix.
[/list]
